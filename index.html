Example Annual Costs

Your annual costs depend on the car you choose and how much you drive.

  • Fuel: €1,200
  • Insurance: €500
  • Maintenance: €400
  • Depreciation: €1,500

Total: €3,600 per year
Average monthly cost: €300
Average cost per kilometer: €0.36

This example does not include parking, tolls, or annual vehicle taxes.

How We Estimated Fuel Costs

Annual fuel cost = (km driven per year ÷ 100) × liters per 100 km × price per liter.

If you drive 10,000 km a year and your car uses 6 liters per 100 km, you need 600 liters of fuel. At €2 per liter, that adds up to €1,200 per year.

How We Estimated Depreciation

Average annual depreciation = (purchase price − resale price) ÷ years owned.

If you buy a car for €12,000 and sell it for €7,500 after three years, it loses €4,500 in value. That's an average of €1,500 per year.

Estimate Your Own Costs

  1. Write down your yearly fuel, insurance, and maintenance costs.
  2. Add the amount you expect your car to lose in value each year.
  3. Include any yearly costs for parking, tolls, and vehicle taxes.
  4. Divide the total by 12 to estimate your monthly cost.
  5. Divide your yearly total by the number of kilometers you drive each year to estimate your cost per kilometer.
